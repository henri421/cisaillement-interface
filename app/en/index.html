Shear along construction joints between concretes cast at different times (slab on precast planks, joint between two pours), to EN 1992-1-1:2004 (6.2.5) or EN 1992-1-1:2023 (8.2.6).

Recommended values of EN 1992-1-1: the National Annex may modify them. The 2nd generation only applies once its National Annex is published.
